About the Artist

Every Piece Starts With a Blank Canvas & a Steady Hand

MK Arts & Design (@_artsbymk) is a one-woman studio painting tote bags, pouches, and accessories entirely by hand — no printers, no templates, no two pieces exactly alike. Each design begins as a sketch and is painted directly onto canvas with fabric paint made to last through wear, sun, and the wash.

Pieces are sold locally at pop-ups and markets around Bahrain — from the Ritz-Carlton Farmer's Market to seasonal Gergaoon events — and shipped by request through Instagram DM.

100% Hand-Painted

No prints or vinyl — every line and petal is painted freehand.

Hand-Washable Paint

Durable, fabric-safe paint that's cured to survive gentle hand-washing.

Premium Canvas

Heavyweight cotton canvas totes and pouches built to actually carry things.

Never Mass-Produced

Small batches and one-of-a-kind commissions — never a factory run.
